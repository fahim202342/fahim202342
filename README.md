## 👨‍💻 About Me

AI/ML engineer and Computer Science student from Bangladesh, focused on building **production-grade** intelligent systems rather than just notebooks. I work across the full stack of modern AI — deep learning & computer vision on one side, and LLMs, RAG, and multi-agent architectures on the other.

**What I bring to the table:**

- 🧠 Strong foundation in Machine Learning, Deep Learning & Computer Vision (CNNs, Transfer Learning, YOLO)
- 🤖 Hands-on experience building multi-agent LLM systems with LangChain, LangGraph, RAG pipelines, and vector databases (ChromaDB, Qdrant)
- 🔐 Backend engineering with FastAPI/Django — JWT auth, RBAC, async SQLAlchemy, PHI/data encryption, audit logging
- 🌏 Building bilingual (Bengali/English) AI systems for real-world, localized use cases
- 🐳 Comfortable shipping — Docker Compose orchestration, deployment pipelines (Flask, Gradio, FastAPI)
- 🚀 Currently deepening expertise in LLMOps and agentic system design

*Always learning, experimenting, and shipping with Artificial Intelligence.*
